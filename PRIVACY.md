# Block Bliss — Privacy Policy

Block Bliss collects **no data**.

- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
- The app never connects to the internet.
- Your game, best score and settings are stored only on your device (and in your device backups). Deleting the app deletes them.
- Crash reports, if any, are only those you choose to share with developers through Apple's own system settings.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/gi-os/BlockBliss/issues
